Automation Project Proposal

Course Companion — AI Knowledge Checks and Weak-Spot Tracking for the Growth Portal

Ad-hoc Activity Plan, Item 4  ·  Prepared by Leander  ·  2026-08-24

Problem

Growth Portal courses today are watch-only. When someone finishes a course, the only signals are a progress percentage and the "I learned something useful" button. Nothing verifies that the viewer actually understood the material, and nothing checks whether it stuck a week later. For the learner that means passive watching with no feedback; for mentors and EMs it means course completion counts that say little about what was actually learned.

Proposed project

A Course Companion that closes the learning loop on every course, using AI where it is strong (generating good questions from a transcript) and keeping a human in the loop where it matters (approving them):

StepWhat happens
1. GenerateA short knowledge check (3–5 questions) is generated from the course transcript using Claude. It stays a draft until the course author or an EM approves it.
2. AnswerThe learner takes the check when they finish the course.
3. ScoreA comprehension score is recorded alongside the existing completion signal.
4. TrackMissed questions land on the learner's personal weak-spot list, tagged by course and concept.
5. ResurfaceWeak spots come back later as a short spaced retest — so the result reflects retention, not just same-day recall.

Why this project

This is the loop my 2-week training plan ran manually, and it worked: a quiz before each build session, every miss logged to a running weak-spot list, and the final retest prepared by drilling that list instead of re-reading everything. The Day 10 retest answers include a question-by-question comparison against the Day 1 baseline. The proposal is simply to automate the mechanism that already produced that result, and make it available on every Growth Portal course — which covers both examples in the activity item: an AI learning activity that speeds up learning, delivered as a GP integration.

Phased scope

PhaseScopeDelivers
MVP Standalone service alongside GP — no GP changes needed. Takes a course transcript, generates a reviewable quiz, serves it, stores scores and the weak-spot list. A working end-to-end demo on one real course, enough to judge question quality and the review workflow.
Phase 1.5 GP integration: the knowledge check appears at course completion; the comprehension score shows on the course card next to progress %. The loop becomes part of the normal course experience.
Phase 2 Mentor/EM view: weak-spot rollups per mentee, plus baseline and retest generation for individual training plans. Automates the assessment side of training plans that is run by hand today.

Risks and mitigations

  • Question quality — AI-generated quizzes can be wrong or trivial. Every quiz is a draft until a human (course author or EM) approves it.
  • How the data is perceived — scores could feel like performance rating. The Companion is scoped as a learning signal, not a performance-rating input: the weak-spot list belongs to the learner, and mentor rollups (phase 2) are mentorship-oriented.
  • Missing transcripts — courses without a transcript get one auto-generated (speech-to-text) during ingestion. Side benefit: transcripts also make the course library searchable.

Proposed next step

If the direction looks right, I would start with the MVP — a small NestJS service using the Claude API, matching the team stack — and demo it on one existing GP course. I estimate the MVP as roughly a one-week time-box at the same daily hours as the course work; I will propose a concrete schedule once the phase-1 scope is agreed.
