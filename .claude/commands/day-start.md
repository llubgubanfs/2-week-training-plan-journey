Start the training day.

1. Read `journal/STATUS.md` and `0-training-plan/training_plan.html`. Determine the current day from STATUS.md, not from today's date.
2. Confirm the day's **prereqs actually exist**. The plan lists prereq chips per day (e.g. Day 4 requires the Day 3 `/metrics` endpoint). Check the filesystem — don't take STATUS.md's word for it. If a prereq is missing, say so before anything else.
3. Report, briefly:
   - Today's objective in one sentence
   - The concrete deliverable Harvey will look at
   - A time budget across the day's tasks that fits ~4 hours, marking what can slip
   - Anything carried over from yesterday
4. Create `journal/day-NN-<slug>.md` from the day's plan entry, with empty sections for what he did, what he learned, and what he got stuck on.
5. **Warm-up quiz: 2–3 questions on yesterday's material**, before any building. Withhold answers until he responds. Log misses to the weak-spot list in STATUS.md.

Then stop and wait. Do not start building — the day's work begins when he's oriented and quizzed.
